17. WHAT THIS BUYS IN SAFETY TERMS (the client's actual priority)
==============================================================================
  exposure hours on a live mine site: 3622 vs 16681 mh (4.6x)
  at a TRIFR of 3.0 per 200,000 h (typical W-African contractor mining norm,
  SRC=ASSUMPTION, not verified): expected recordables 0.05 vs 0.25
  field welds in HCN service: 44 (modular) vs ~157 (stick-built)
  every field weld avoided = one fewer lethal-service leak path made under a
  tarpaulin in 32 C / 85% RH instead of in a climate-controlled shop with an
  automatic orbital welder and a permanent RT bay.
  THIS is the safety argument for modular, and it is quantitative:
    113 Cat-M field joints eliminated.
