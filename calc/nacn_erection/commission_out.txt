9. DRYING AND INERTING THE HCN CIRCUIT
==========================================================================
   reactor/burner D1.05x3.0    2.60 m3
   WHB shell + quench          2.00 m3
   NH3 absorber D0.8x10        5.03 m3
   HCN absorber D0.8x9         4.52 m3
   thermal oxidiser            4.00 m3
   scrubbers 2x                6.00 m3
   DN250 x20 m                 0.98 m3
   DN200 x40 m                 1.26 m3
   small-bore + relief hdr     1.20 m3
   HCN-wetted volume = 27.6 m3; +25% dead legs/jackets = 34.5 m3
dilution purge (perfect mixing): n = ln(C0/C) = ln(20.9/0.5) = 3.73 volume changes
real geometry (dead legs, gauze pack, packed columns) -> use n = 7
N2 required = 34.5 x 7 = 241 Nm3 = 302 kg
  = 0.15 standard 50 L/200 bar packs -> ONE liquid-N2 dewar
  (180 kg LN2 = 145 Nm3) covers a purge; keep 2 on site + a 500 L bulk vessel for
  the continuous seal/analyser purge (est. 4-8 Nm3/h -> 100-200 Nm3/day).
dry-out after hydrotest: 20.0 kg residual water; sweep gas picks up 0.005 kg H2O/kg N2
  N2 = 20.0/0.005 = 4000 kg = 3200 Nm3; at 100 Nm3/h -> 32 h = 1.3 days
  WHY IT MATTERS: residual water + HCN + alkali -> HCN polymerisation (black
  polymer) and hydrolysis to formamide/formate. Wet start-up fouls the gauze
  and the absorber packing on day one. Dry-out is NOT optional and NOT fast.
  Mitigation: pneumatic-test the Cat-M gas circuit instead of hydro where B31.3
  345.5 permits -> removes 32 h from the critical path (but 345.5 needs a written
  hazard review; Cat M pneumatic testing also needs owner's approval).

11. HOT COMMISSIONING SEQUENCE (Andrussow specifics)
==========================================================================
   N2 leak/tightness at operating P, gas detection live         8 h
   air blower + oxidiser on fuel gas only, stack in service    16 h
   caustic circulation M3/M6, pH loop tuned to >11.5, deluge test  24 h
   Pt/Rh gauze pack installed (last item - $155k, theft/damage)   6 h
   start-up burner light, gauze preheat to ~600 C on CH4/air   10 h
   NH3 cut-in, ratio ramp to Andrussow mix, LIGHT-OFF ~1100 C   8 h
   gauze conditioning ('cauliflower' restructuring), yield climbs  48 h
   ramp 40%->70%->100% load, 3 plateaus x 12 h                 36 h
   stabilise, tune, snag close-out before guarantee run        24 h
   subtotal hot commissioning = 180 h = 7.5 days elapsed (24/7 shift work)
   NOTE: gauze conditioning (48 h) is why a 72 h guarantee run cannot start on
   the day of light-off - HCN yield on a fresh Pt/Rh gauze is below steady state.

12. GUARANTEE / PERFORMANCE TEST
==========================================================================
     72 h run ->   22.5 t NaCN(100%) =   75.0 t of 30% solution
    168 h run ->   52.5 t NaCN(100%) =  175.0 t of 30% solution
    720 h run ->  225.0 t NaCN(100%) =  750.0 t of 30% solution
   72 h @ nameplate is the standard mechanical/performance guarantee.
   For a SAFETY-BENCHMARK plant add a 30-day AVAILABILITY test (720 h): it is the
   only test that exposes trip-rate, scrubber saturation and unmanned-night
   behaviour. Cost of that: 225 t NaCN(100%) - which the mine consumes anyway.
   KEY PLUG-AND-PLAY ADVANTAGE: there is NO off-spec disposal problem. Commissioning
   liquor at any strength goes into the leach circuit provided pH>11.5 and free-CN
   is assayed. The customer IS the sink. (Contrast: a merchant plant must dispose.)
