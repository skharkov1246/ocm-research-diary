5. MODULE SETTING - CRANE SIZING AND LIFT CYCLE
==========================================================================
heaviest module M5 (e-house) = 21.0 t; spreader beam 2.5 t; rigging 0.6 t
gross load = (21.0+2.5+0.6) x DAF 1.25 = 30.1 t
set radius 14.0 m (crane hardstand outside bund), utilisation limit 75%
required chart capacity = 30.1/0.75 = 40.2 t @ 14.0 m
  -> 130-160 t class all-terrain (e.g. LTM 1130-5.1 / LTM 1160-5.2) on main boom.
  -> a 100 t class is marginal at 14 m: DO NOT plan on it. SRC=ASSUMPTION (no
     load chart retrieved this session); confirm against actual chart + ground
     bearing pressure (outrigger pads on 40 t/m2 min, Tarkwa laterite needs mats).
lift cycle = 0.75 rig + 0.50 lift&land + 1.25 shim/survey/derig = 2.5 h
lifts = 6 modules + 6 tanks/silo + 3 stack + 4 misc = 19
crane hours = 19 x 2.5 = 48 h -> at 9 h/day = 5.3 crane-days
  + 1 d mobilise/assemble + 1 d demobilise -> book the crane for 5 days.
setting tolerance: +-6 mm elevation, +-10 mm plan (else the 44 Cat-M spool
  fit-ups go out of tolerance and become field re-cuts).
grout: non-shrink cementitious, 24-48 h before load transfer, 7 d before hydrotest
  of module-supported piping -> grout cure is ON the critical path, 3 d practical.
